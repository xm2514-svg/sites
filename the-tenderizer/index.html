The Tenderizer — Najena has two of them

Two named, one name, two rooms and two loot tables. Which one has the haste belt, and where each one stands.

There are two of them

This is the thing that wastes people's time in Najena. Two different named share the name The Tenderizer, at two different levels, in two different rooms, with completely different loot.

Level/locDrops
22(131,342)Kitchen Toolbelt (Haste +10%), The Tenderizer (weapon)
25(135,287)Sword of the Lost, Signet Ring of the Lost Crusader

Which one you want

If you came for haste, you want the level 22 one at (131,342). Its Kitchen Toolbelt is the earliest haste item a trio can realistically wear, and haste is the single biggest DPS jump available at that level.

The level 25 one at (135,287) is a different fight and a different prize. Neither drop is a long-term keeper for a Paladin / Monk / Shaman trio, but both merge.

Fitting it into a run

Najena is a loop zone. Take the level 22 Tenderizer on the way in, Drelzna at (-35,-222) for the Bloodstained and Golden Crescent keys, and Unbound Flame at (-26,360) for the Flaming Fist. The full named list with levels and coordinates is on the Najena named loot page.
