Master Crushbone Cell Key — Orc Warden (107,435)

Who actually drops the Master Crushbone Cell Key, what it opens, and why you cannot be handed one.

Who drops it

SourceLevel/loc
Orc Warden15(107,435)
orc slaver (trash)—slaver caves

The Orc Warden is the reliable source and he respawns quickly since the launch patches. The orc slaver trash mobs in the caves can also drop it, which is why some players never notice a named is involved at all.

What it opens

The slaver cells in Crushbone, levels 4-10. This is the part worth knowing: Crushbone genuinely has locked doors, and almost no source documents them — most guides describe the zone as if you could simply walk through.

Flags

FlagsNO DROP, quest item
Weight / Size0.0 / Tiny
Classes / RacesAll

Being NO DROP, nobody can hand you one. Every character who wants through those cells farms their own.

While you are there

Crushbone was rebuilt in the 14 July 2026 patch (levels 4-22) with guaranteed named and no placeholders, and the Orc Warden's respawn was shortened again at launch. The same trip gives you the Bracers of Battle off the Warden, the Shiny Brass Shield from the Orc Trainer on Trainer Hill, and the Brass Earring from the Taskmaster — two thirds of the Screaming Mace quest.
