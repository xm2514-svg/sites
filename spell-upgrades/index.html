Spell upgrade tiers, +1 to +10 — the mote cost and what it buys

Rank by rank. Each rank costs twice the previous one in motes — 2 at +1, 32 at +5, 256 at +8, 1 024 to reach +10 — and gives a direct-damage spell +6 % of its base damage, a buff +10 % duration, and every spell a shorter cast and a lower mana cost. Drag the slider above to read any spell at any tier.

Where the numbers come from

The game's own files. Spell data is read from the client's spells_us.txt and dbstr_us.txt — the same files the game reads to draw your spell window. Not a wiki, not a scrape. What the client shows is a base value grown by your level and then capped: value = base + level × factor, capped at the maximum. That is why the same spell reads differently on two characters, and why community databases quoting a single number are right for exactly one level.
The per-tier rates were measured on a live client on 25 August 2026, not copied from a table: cast −2 % per rank on direct-damage spells and −4 % on everything else, mana −2 % on anything that deals damage or heals and −4 % on buffs and pets, recovery and reuse −2 %, duration +10 % on buffs and debuffs and +5 % on damage- and heal-over-time, and a mote cost that doubles every rank to 1 024 at rank 10. Linear, not compounding.
A tier never raises damage. Blizzard Blast merged for real from tier 0 to tier 1 still reads 346. Envenomed Bolt, whose ticks are nowhere near their cap, reads 345 – 351 at tier 0 and at tier 4 alike. What grows is the duration: 36 s becomes 42 s, six ticks become seven, so the spell lands +16 % total damage while every hit is unchanged. Ticks are floored — 6 × 1.20 = 7.2 gives seven, not 7.2.
Every rank also cuts the resist check by 15. Odium shows Resist: Magic −60 at tier 4, Blizzard Blast Resist: Cold −15 at tier 1. The spell window never mentions it before you place the mote, and on a resisted line it may be worth more than the mana it saves.
Two things the spell window never shows. A pet gains a level per tier — Frenzied Spirit summons a level 36 wolf at +0, a level 41 one at tier 5. And on crowd control, a rank raises the highest NPC level the spell will land on.
Reading your own window. The value in parentheses is the fully modified one: it already carries your stance, specialisation and focus items, so a stat can read modified at tier 0. Compare against your own tier 0, not against the base.
