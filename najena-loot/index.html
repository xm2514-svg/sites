What is actually worth your time

  • Unbound Flame (-26,360) — Flaming Fist. Farm two if you are a Monk.
  • The Tenderizer (131,342) — Kitchen Toolbelt, Haste +10%. Your first haste item, and it arrives around level 22.
  • Najena herself (353,105) — Clawed Knuckle-Ring and Flowing Black Robe.
  • Drelzna (-35,-222) — carries the Bloodstained Key and the Golden Crescent Key, which open the rest of the zone.

Run it as a loop, not a camp

Najena is a revamped zone: guaranteed named, no placeholders. Sitting on one spawn wastes the whole design. Clear the named in a fixed circuit and they have respawned by the time you come back round — every lap pays.

The zone carries a trio comfortably from 15 to 22, and the keys mean you should do Drelzna early rather than last.
