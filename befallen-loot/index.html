The complete Befallen loot table with coordinates, and the four named actually worth building a circuit around.

Every named in Befallen

Befallen is a revamped zone: the named are guaranteed, there are no placeholders to break through. That changes how you play it — you run a circuit and come back, you do not sit on a spawn.

The four that matter

  • Baron Telyx V`Zher (28) — Pristine Studded Leather Tunic, AC 14 chest, and The Baron's Blade.
  • Korven Nisere (24) — Shimmering Ruby Stiletto.
  • Knight V`Tal (24) — the Enchanted Fine Steel Bastard Sword, a genuine Paladin weapon for the level.
  • Boondin Babbinsbort (25) — Icon of the Ardent, the Gauntlets of the Ardent turn-in.

The studded set

Four named carry pieces of the Pristine Studded Leather set — Arisen Thaumaturgist the bracer, Footman of V`Zher the gloves, Soldier of V`Zher the leggings and sleeves, Baron Telyx the tunic. Collecting it is the reason a trio stays in this zone rather than passing through.

Where it fits in a climb

Befallen carries a trio from about 10 to 15 for gear and stays worth running into the low twenties for merge copies and motes. It is the first zone out of the Commonlands where the loot is worth planning around.
