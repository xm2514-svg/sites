Motes of Potential — all 10 ranks and what each one can actually feed

Every mote in EverQuest Legends, the maximum tier it can feed, and the reason a mote suddenly does nothing on an item you are still upgrading.

The ten motes, and where each one stops

A mote gives a fixed amount of merge experience, but it also has a maximum tier it can feed. Past that tier the mote does nothing on that item — it is not reduced, it is simply refused. This is the single most misunderstood part of the system, and the reason people think their upgrades stalled.

Why your Infinitesimal motes stopped working

A Mote of Infinitesimal Potential feeds tier 0 only. The moment your item reaches tier 1 it is dead weight. That is working as intended — you are meant to move up the ladder as the item climbs.

Once an item is past what your best mote can feed, the only way to push it further is duplicate gear: merging another copy of the item itself. High-tier items are finished with copies, never with motes.

Where motes drop

Mostly from named, and infrequently from trash. Three things raise what you get:

  • Zone difficulty raises the minimum mote tier that can drop.
  • Killing yellow and red cons raises the drop chance.
  • Your own level caps it — roughly one mote tier per 5 levels: level 15 → tier 3, level 35 → tier 7, level 50 → tier 10.

Motes can themselves be upgraded.

The practical consequence for a farming route: the named you already kill for gear are your mote supply. You do not need a separate mote farm — you need a named loop you can run fast and repeatedly.

The trick the developers demonstrated

In the official EQ Legends Insights episode on item merging, the executive producer does not demonstrate the system on a raid item. He farms dervish cutthroats in the North Desert of Ro — a newbie zone — for a plain Cutthroat Insignia Ring (AC 1, DEX 2), merges copies up to +10, and ends with AC 11 and DEX 12.

The limit on a tier 10 item is not rarity — it is how many copies you can farm. An item that drops constantly in an easy zone can finish stronger than a rare you will see three times in your whole climb.

Two details from the same demo: at Difficulty 4 the ring dropped at +4 or better straight away, and merging a +1 copy added 2 points to the bar — because that +1 was itself built from two +0 rings. Merged gear carries its history forward.
