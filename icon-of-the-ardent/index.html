Icon of the Ardent — quest turn-in, Befallen (-814,-206)

No stats, no slot: what the Icon of the Ardent is actually for, and which named in Befallen carries it.

It is a quest item, not a stat item

The Icon of the Ardent has no stats at all — no AC, no attributes, no slot. If you are looking at one in your bags wondering what it does: on its own, nothing. It is a turn-in.

Boondin Babbinsbort25(-814,-206)Befallen

He also drops a plain Dagger. The name is spelled Babbinsbort in the atlas data and Babbinsport on the wiki — same named, both spellings turn him up.

It is No Drop, so it cannot be bought or traded: the only way to hold one is to kill him yourself.

Getting there

He sits at (-814,-206) on the lower level of Befallen, in the same stretch as the level 24-28 knights. Befallen is a revamped zone — guaranteed named, no placeholders — so he is there every cycle and the run is worth chaining with Korven Nisere and Baron Telyx V`Zher rather than camping him alone.
