Which of Najena's two Tenderizers actually drops the belt, and why wearing a second haste item does nothing.

The part that costs people gear slots

Haste does not stack with itself. Within the worn-haste category only the highest single item counts. A second haste item does nothing at all.

So the Kitchen Toolbelt at +10% is an early-game belt, and it is replaced outright the day you get an FBSS at +21% — not worn alongside it. Worn haste does stack with spell haste (a Shaman's Quickness) and with a Monk's discipline, which is why a trio still wants the belt on the Monk while the Shaman buffs.

Getting it without camping

Najena rewards a loop, not a camp. The Tenderizer at (131,342), Drelzna at (-35,-222) for her two keys, then Unbound Flame at (-26,360) for the Flaming Fist. By the time you are back at the start the first named has respawned. The belt and a Monk's two fists come out of the same circuit — the whole named list is on the Najena named loot page.
